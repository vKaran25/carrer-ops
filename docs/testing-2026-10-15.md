### Issues exposed

- The Chennai query says `in Chennai`, but the captured parser output included a preferred 50 km scope with evidence `in Chennai`. Plain city queries must remain strict; current validation allows this incorrect scope. This is a parser/validation defect.
- London, Berlin and Toronto capture failures were `GeographyError`. Bare London/Berlin are ambiguous in the offline dataset; country-qualified London/United Kingdom and Berlin/Germany resolve. A separate London parser rerun returned strict locations and succeeded. The capture stores exception classes only, so its precise failing stage/payload was not retained; location-scope output is inconsistent and needs investigation without guessing a country.
- The Ahmedabad result's structured location says Ahmedabad, while its captured JD explicitly says `Work Location: Surat/ Remote`. This source inconsistency needs handling before claiming mandatory-location accuracy.
- A Pune scored result has only a short secondary-site description. Structured JobPosting markup does not by itself establish a complete original-employer JD; description completeness/provenance needs review.
- Junior queries returned senior roles among weak matches. Seniority is currently parsed as a soft preference, so this is a relevance finding rather than proof of a hard-filter bypass. Independent relevance review remains pending.
- Most eligible results came from a small set of retrieved pages despite over 1,100 postings retrieved per completed case. City/role retrieval and filtering require further evaluation; raw board volume does not establish Indian coverage.
