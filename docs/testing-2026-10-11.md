## Manual checks with your own resume

1. Open http://127.0.0.1:5173. In Settings, verify the selected Cohere model, PDF compiler availability and local learning off.
2. Select Keywords and search `Python`. Check returned titles, companies and links against the real postings. Coverage is the enabled configured sources.
3. Select Natural language and search `Find Python jobs worldwide`. Inspect the matching explanation. Impossible mandatory filters can correctly return no jobs.
4. Upload a text-readable `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.txt`, or self-contained `.tex` resume, maximum 5 MB. Compare every extracted fact with the original before using any draft. Report unsupported or changed claims.
5. Search `Jobs matching my resume`. Compare match explanations with your stored facts and the actual descriptions.
6. Open one job and request Tailor. Review its draft and critic. Claims must come from your stored statements; other jobs should not be tailored automatically.
7. Switch the tracker between Kanban and list, then reload. Check job/draft persistence. Record a job as applied only if you actually applied; the automated isolated test already covered that gate.
8. Curation and interview research still have failing live acceptance. Check every output before relying on them; blocked drafts and insufficient-information responses remain real limitations.
